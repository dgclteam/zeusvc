1 Identity, Scope, and Third-Party Websites

This Privacy Policy describes how Digital Codec Labs ("we", "us", or "the developer") handles information in connection with your use of the Zeus Video Caster mobile application (hereinafter, "the Application", including any of its trade names, versions, and updates).

The Application operates exclusively as a web browser and a technological tool for streaming multimedia content in real time to compatible external displays. The Application does not host, provide, or index its own multimedia content, nor does it allow downloading copyrighted files.

Important Notice Regarding Web Browsing: This Privacy Policy applies exclusively to the Application. When using the built-in browser, you will access third-party websites that are neither operated nor controlled by us. Those websites may use their own cookies, trackers, and privacy policies. We strongly advise you to review the privacy policies of any website you visit, as we have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content or privacy practices of third parties.

2 Data Stored Locally on Your Device

By design, we do not maintain user accounts or operate servers that collect or store your browsing activity. The following information is saved solely and exclusively in the internal memory of your own mobile device to provide you with a seamless experience:

  • Your bookmarked or favorite web pages.
  • Your history of visited websites.
  • Your search query history.
  • Your history of detected or played videos.
  • Standard cookies and session data generated by the system's web browsing component when visiting third-party sites.

We do not have access to this information. You have full control over these records and can delete them at any time from the Application's settings menu or by clearing the Application's storage data in your Android system settings.

3 Media Streaming, Local Network, and Shared Files

To enable TV discovery and smooth real-time video streaming, the Application performs the following operations strictly locally:

  • Local Streaming Bridge: Many web video streams require specific browser connection parameters to play properly on a television. To resolve this, the Application creates an internal communication bridge within your own phone that forwards the video stream directly to your TV over your home Wi-Fi network. No video fragment, media link, or web address ever passes through Digital Codec Labs servers.
  • Device Discovery and Background Playback: The Application uses your local Wi-Fi connection to scan for compatible TVs and media receivers within your same home network. Local network addresses of your TVs are only used temporarily in your device's volatile memory during the active session. Additionally, while casting a video, the Application keeps an active playback service with media controls in your notification bar to prevent the operating system from interrupting the stream when your phone screen turns off.
  • Files Shared from Your Gallery: The Application does not browse, scan, or request general access to your device's folders or storage. If you voluntarily choose to share an individual media file from another app (such as your photo gallery) to Zeus Video Caster using your phone's "Share" or "Open with" feature, the Application only reads that specific file to stream it in real time to your TV over your local Wi-Fi network, without copying it or uploading it to the internet.

4 Search Services, Trends, and Browsing Filters

The Application relies on specific external services to offer discovery features and optimize web browsing:

  • Search and Trending Services: When you browse the trending section or perform a search within the Application, your device connects directly to an external search and content discovery service provider (without passing through Digital Codec Labs servers). To deliver results and trends tailored to your language and region, as well as to validate connection security and prevent automated abuse, the Application transmits the search query along with basic technical environment parameters (device brand and model, operating system version, language, country, and time zone) and random technical identifiers generated locally by the Application. These identifiers are not linked to your personal identity or physical hardware serial numbers, and they are completely reset if you clear the Application's data or uninstall it.
  • Technical Configuration and Internal Browsing Filter: To ensure proper operation of the Application and improve the reading experience exclusively within our built-in web browser, the Application periodically downloads technical configuration parameters and web element filtering rule lists from external repositories. These automated downloads are anonymous, do not transmit personal user data, and the filter operates strictly within the Application's web viewer without interfering with other apps or your device's operating system.

The Application is free to use and is supported by advertising, relying on the following services provided by Google LLC:

  • Google AdMob: We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within the Application. To serve relevant ads (personalized or contextual, depending on your consent choice), limit how often you see the same ad, compile ad performance statistics, and detect advertising fraud, the AdMob service may automatically collect and process:
    • Your device's Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID).
    • Your IP address (used to estimate a general, non-precise geographic location and for security purposes).
    • Ad interaction metrics (ad views and clicks) and technical/diagnostic device information (manufacturer, model, carrier, language, and OS version).
  • Google Cast Services (Google Play Services): Used to enable detection and communication with Chromecast devices and TVs with built-in Google Cast technology.

Data processing by Google is governed by its own Privacy Policy, available at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID and opt out of personalized advertising at any time from your Android phone settings (Settings > Google > All services > Ads) or through the consent management options available within the Application (depending on your region).
